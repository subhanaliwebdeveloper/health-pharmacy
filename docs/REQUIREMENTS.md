# Requirements coverage

The supplied visual/design requirements are mapped to:
- Green/teal pharmacy branding, Poppins headings and Open Sans body.
- Responsive home page with hero, featured products, benefits and offers.
- Medicines/product listing with categories, search and sorting.
- Product detail with price, stock, rating, prescription flag and add-to-cart.
- Shopping cart, delivery fee, checkout and order success.
- Login/signup and customer account.
- About and contact pages.
- Admin panel for products, orders, users, prescriptions, payments, coupons, reviews, reports and settings.
- MySQL entities: Users, Products, Orders, Order_Items plus Categories, Prescriptions, Coupons and Reviews.
